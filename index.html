03About

Logic and creativity, on the same plan.

Hi, I’m Sydney Vo, a senior at Meredith College pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Interior Design with minors in Marketing and Studio Arts.

My passion for design began early, working on creative projects with my dad, and my background in STEM shaped a mindset that bridges logical structure with creative vision. I believe good design must be inherently accessible and inclusive, so I work to create environments that are intuitive, highly functional, and welcoming for everyone.

Through internships at Furnitureland South, Lauren Ashley Design LLC, and MM Interior Design Group, I’ve built a strong foundation in AutoCAD, SketchUp, Enscape, visual storytelling, and site coordination — and I keep learning through new digital tools, creative crafts, and sharper technical skills.

  1. 01

    Accessible by default

    Dual-height counters, clear circulation, and ADA-compliant planning are part of the concept, not an afterthought.

  2. 02

    Structure meets vision

    A STEM background shapes how I organize a plan — then material, light, and color make it feel human.

  3. 03

    Always learning

    From hand drafting to Revit and Enscape, I keep adding tools, crafts, and technical skills to the toolkit.
